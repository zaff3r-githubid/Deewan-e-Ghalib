Sab kahan kuchh lala-o-gul mein numayan ho gayin

سب کہاں کچھ لالہ و گل میں نمایاں ہو گئیں

By Mirza Ghalib

Couplet 1
سبکہاںکچھلالہوگلمیںنمایاںہوگئیں
خاکمیںکیاصورتیںہوںگیکہپنہاںہوگئیں
Sab kahan kuchh lala-o-gul mein numayan ho gayin
Khaak mein kya soortain hongi ke pinhan ho gayin
Where are all those forms? A few appeared in the tulip and rose;
What countenances must be in the dust, that they became hidden!

Couplet 2
یاںنفسہاپردۂاوہامہیںاےبےخودی
واںحجابِخودنگاہیسےمژہداںہوگئیں
Yaan nafas-ha parda-e-auhaam hain ae be-khudi
Waan hijaab-e-khud-nigahi se mizah-daan ho gayin
Here, breaths are veils of delusions, O self-oblivion;
There, from the veil of self-gazing, eyelashes became a trap.

Couplet 3
قیدمیںیعقوبنےلیتھینہیوسفکیخبر
لیکنآنکھیںروزَنِدیوارِزنداںہوگئیں
Qaid mein Yaqoob ne li thi na Yusuf ki khabar
Lekin aankhen rozan-e-deewar-e-zindan ho gayin
In his confinement, Yaqoob did not receive news of Yusuf;
But his eyes became the windows in the prison wall.

Couplet 4
وہبھیابمجھسےعداوتکےگماںمیںہوگئے
ہاںرفیقِشبِغممیرےجوزنداںہوگئے
Woh bhi ab mujh se adawat ke gumaan mein ho gaye
Haan rafeeq-e-shab-e-gham mere jo zindan ho gaye
Even they now harbor suspicions of enmity towards me;
Yes, my companions in the night of sorrow, who became my prison.

Couplet 5
ہاںوہمشقِرفوگریمیںتوہشیارتھامیر
چاہیےیہبھیکہاسسےرگِجاںہوگئیں
Haan woh mashq-e-rafoogari mein to hoshiyar tha Mir
Chahiye yeh bhi ke us se rag-e-jaan ho gayin
Yes, Mir was skilled in the practice of darning;
This too is required, that from it, the veins of life became [visible/repaired].
