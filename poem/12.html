Ab mujhe dekh ke aa'īna-e-qadr-e-gulshan

اب مجھے دیکھ کے آئینۂ قدرِ گلشن

By Mirza Ghalib

Couplet 1
ابمجھےدیکھکےآئینۂقدرِگلشن
ایکعالمہےکہسرگرمِتماشاہےمرا
Ab mujhe dekh ke aa'īna-e-qadr-e-gulshan
Ek 'ālam hai ki sargarm-e-tamāsha hai mirā
Now that it has seen me, the mirror of the garden's worth,
A whole world is busy in witnessing my spectacle.
