Bus Ke Dushwar Hai Har Kaam Ka Aasan Hona

بس کہ دشوار ہے ہر کام کا آساں ہونا

By Mirza Ghalib

Couplet 1
بسکہدشوارہےہرکامکاآساںہونا
آدمیکوبھیمَیسَّرنہیںاِنساںہونا
Bus ke dushwar hai har kaam ka aasaan hona
Aadmi ko bhi mayassar nahin insaan hona
It is so difficult for every task to become easy
That it's not even granted to a human to become humane.

Couplet 2
گِریَہچاہےہےخرابیمرےکاشانےکی
درودیوارسےٹپکےہےبیاباںہونا
Girya chaahe hai kharaabi mere kaashaane ki
Dar-o-deewaar se tapke hai bayaabaan hona
My weeping desires the ruin of my dwelling
From its doors and walls drips the essence of a wilderness.

Couplet 3
واےدیوانگیِشوقکہہردممجھکو
آپجانااُدھراورآپہیحیراںہونا
Waae deewaanagi-e-shauq ki har dam mujh ko
Aap jaana udhar aur aap hi hairaan hona
Alas, the madness of desire, that every moment for me
I myself go there, and I myself am astonished.

Couplet 4
عیشعشرتکیتمنانہدلکوچھیڑےہے
اےخوشاوہکہمیسرنہیںارماںہونا
Aish-ishrat ki tamanna na dil ko chhede hai
Ae khushaa woh ke mayassar nahin armaan hona
The desire for comfort and pleasure does not stir my heart
Oh, how fortunate is he for whom no desires are attainable.

Couplet 5
عِشرتِقطرہہےدریامیںفناہوجانا
دردکاحدسےگُزرناہےدواہوجانا
Ishrat-e-qatra hai darya mein fanaa ho jaana
Dard ka hadd se guzarna hai dawa ho jaana
The joy of a drop lies in becoming annihilated in the river
The passing of pain beyond its limits is to become a cure.

Couplet 6
غمِہستیکااسدؔکسسےہوجُزمَرگعلاج
شمعہررنگمیںجلتیہےسحرہوجانا
Gham-e-hasti ka Asadؔ kis se ho juz marg ilaaj
Shama har rang mein jalti hai sahar ho jaana
Oh Asad, who can cure the sorrow of existence, save for death?
The candle burns in every color, only to become the dawn.
