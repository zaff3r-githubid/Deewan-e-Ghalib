Aah ko chahiye ik umr asar hone tak

آہ کو چاہیئے اِک عُمر اثر ہونے تک

By Mirza Ghalib

Couplet 1
آہکوچاہیئےاِکعُمراثرہونےتک
کونجیتاہےتِریزُلفکےسَرہونےتک
Aah ko chahiye ik umr asar hone tak
Kaun jeeta hai tiri zulf ke sar hone tak
A sigh needs a lifetime to take effect,
Who lives long enough for your tresses to be tamed?

Couplet 2
دامہرموجمیںہےحلقۂصدکامِنہنگ
دیکھیںکیاگزریہےقطرےپہگُہرہونےتک
Daam har mauj mein hai halqa-e sad kaam-e nahang
Dekhein kya guzri hai qatre pe guhar hone tak
In every wave's trap lies the hundred-toothed jaw of a crocodile,
Let's see what a drop endures until it becomes a pearl.

Couplet 3
عاشقیصَبرطلباورتمَنّابیداد
جیکاکیاکامہےاِسراہمیںسَرہونےتک
Aashiqi sabr talab aur tamanna bedaad
Jee ka kya kaam hai is raah mein sar hone tak
Love demands patience, and desire is tyrannical,
What business does the heart have in this path, until it's sacrificed?

Couplet 4
ہمنےماناکہتَغافُلنہکروگےلیکن
خاکہوجائیںگےہمتمکوخَبَرہونےتک
Hum ne mana ke taghaful na karoge lekin
Khaak ho jayenge hum tum ko khabar hone tak
We admit you won't be neglectful, but
We will turn to dust by the time you become aware.

Couplet 5
اِکنظربیشنہیںفُرصتِہستیغافل
گرمیِبزمہےاِکرقصِشررہونےتک
Ik nazar besh nahin fursat-e hasti ghafil
Garmi-e bazm hai ik raqs-e sharar hone tak
Life's opportunity, O heedless one, is no more than a single glance,
The warmth of the gathering lasts only as long as a spark's dance.

Couplet 6
غمِہستیکااسدکسسےہوجُزوَےکہبجُز
مرگاِکچارہنہیںآہسحرہونےتک
Gham-e hasti ka Asad kis se ho juzv-e ke bajuz
Marg ik charah nahin aah-e sahar hone tak
O Asad, who can share a part of this grief of existence, when besides
Death, there is no remedy until the morning sigh?
