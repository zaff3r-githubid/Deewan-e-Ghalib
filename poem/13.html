Woh firaq aur woh visal kahan

وہ فراق اور وہ وصال کہاں

By Mirza Ghalib

Couplet 1
وہفراقاوروہوصالکہاں
وہشبوروزوماہوسالکہاں
Woh firaq aur woh visal kahan
Woh shab-o-roz-o-maah-o-saal kahan
Where is that separation and where is that union?
Where are those nights and days, months and years?

Couplet 2
تھیوہاکشخصکےتصورسے
ابوہرعنائیِخیالکہاں
Thi woh ik shakhs ke tasavvur se
Ab woh ra'na'i-e khayal kahan
It was due to the thought of a certain person;
Where is that beauty of imagination now?

Couplet 3
رہگئیرسمِاذاں،روحِبلال
وہنمازاوروہکمالکہاں
Reh gai rasm-e-azan, rooh-e-Bilal
Woh namaz aur woh kamal kahan
Only the ritual of the call to prayer remains, the spirit of Bilal is gone;
Where is that prayer and that perfection now?

Couplet 4
تھاوہہنگامہدربزمِہستی
وہہمآغوشیِخیالکہاں
Tha woh hungama dar bazm-e-hasti
Woh hum-aghoshī-e-khayal kahan
There was once that commotion in the assembly of existence;
Where is that embrace of thoughts now?

We once had thoughts of you, similar to those;
Where is that embrace of images now?

Couplet 6
جبوہآئیںگےتوکیادیکھیںگے
ابتوویرانہیںمکانکہاں
Jab woh aayenge toh kya dekhenge
Ab toh veeraan hain makaan kahan
When they come, what will they see?
Now the dwellings are desolate, where are those?

Couplet 7
بختِبرگشتہکیشکایتکیا
وہزمانہوہدلیوہحالکہاں
Bakht-e-bargashata ki shikayat kya
Woh zamana woh Dilli woh haal kahan
What complaint is there of reversed fortune?
Where is that era, that Delhi, that condition now?
