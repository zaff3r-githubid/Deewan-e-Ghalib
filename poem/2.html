Hazaaron khwahishein aisi ke har khwahish pe dam nikle

ہزاروں خواہشیں ایسی کہ ہر خواہش پہ دم نکلے

By Mirza Ghalib

Couplet 1
ہزاروںخواہشیںایسیکہہرخواہشپہدمنکلے
بہتنکلےمرےارمانلیکنپھربھیکمنکلے
Hazaaron khwahishein aisi ke har khwahish pe dam nikle
Bahut nikle mere armaan lekin phir bhi kam nikle
Thousands of desires, such that each desire makes life depart (takes my breath away).
Many of my aspirations were fulfilled, yet they still felt few.

Couplet 2
ڈرےکیوںمیراقاتل،کیارہےگااسکیگردنپر
وہخوںجسکےکہدعوےدارہوںفرہادوجمشیدبھی
Dare kyon mera qaatil, kya rahega uski gardan par
Woh khoon jiske ke daavedaar hon Farhad-o-Jamshed bhi
Why should my killer fear, what burden will remain on their neck?
That blood, for which even Farhad and Jamshed are claimants.

Couplet 3

We have always heard of Adam being expelled from paradise, but
We left your street greatly disgraced.

Couplet 4

Bharam khul jaaye zaalim! teri qaamat ki daraazi ka
Agar us turra-e pur pech o kham ka pech o kham nikle
O cruel one, the illusion of your stature's height would be revealed,
If the twists and turns of that intricate lock of hair were to unravel.

But if anyone wants to get a letter written to her, they should get it written by me.
Morning broke, and I left home with a pen tucked behind my ear.

Couplet 6
ہوئیاسدورمیںمنسوبمجھسےبادہآشامی

Hui is daur mein mansoob mujhse baada-aashami

In this era, wine-drinking has been attributed to me.
Then, O Lord, why should I not feel sorrow for this unjust defamation?

Couplet 7

Those from whom I expected appreciation for my suffering,
They turned out to be more wounded than me by the sword of cruelty.

Couplet 8
محبتمیںنہیںہےفرقجینےاورمرنےکا
اسیکودیکھکرجیتےہیںجسکافرپہدمنکلے
Mohabbat mein nahin hai farq jeene aur marne ka
Usi ko dekh kar jeete hain jis kaafir pe dam nikle
In love, there is no difference between living and dying.
We live by gazing at the very infidel for whom we would die.

Couplet 9
ذراکرزورسینےپرکہتیرِپُرستمنکلے
جواُسنامہرباںکےسینےمیںہے،سووہکمنکلے
Zara kar zor seene par ki teer-e pur-sitam nikle
Jo us na-mehrbaan ke seene mein hai, so woh kam nikle
Exert a little force on your chest, so that the arrow full of cruelty might emerge.
May what is in the chest of that unkind one turn out to be less (or be removed).

Couplet 10
خداکےواسطےپردہنہکعبےسےاٹھاظالم!
کہیںایسانہہویاںبھیوہیکافرصنمنکلے
Khuda ke waaste parda na Kaabe se utha zaalim!
Kahin aisa na ho yaan bhi wahi kaafir sanam nikle
For God's sake, O cruel one, do not lift the veil from the Kaaba!
Lest it happen that here too, the same infidel idol appears.

Couplet 11
کہاںمیخانےکادروازہ'غالب'اورکہاںواعظ
پراتناجانتےہیںکلوہجاتاتھاکہہمنکلے
Kahaan maikhaane ka darwaaza 'Ghalib' aur kahaan waaiz
Par itna jaante hain kal woh jaata tha ke hum nikle
Where is the door of the tavern, Ghalib, and where is the preacher?
But this much we know, yesterday he was leaving when we emerged.
