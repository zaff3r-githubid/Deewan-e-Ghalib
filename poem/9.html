Baazeecha-e-atfaal hai duniya mere aage

بازیچہ اطفال ہے دنیا مرے آگے

By Mirza Ghalib

Couplet 1
بازیچۂاطفالہےدنیامرےآگے
ہوتاہےشبوروزتماشامرےآگے
Baazeecha-e-atfaal hai duniya mere aage
Hota hai shab-o-roz tamasha mere aage
The world is a children's playground before me,
A spectacle unfolds day and night before me.

Couplet 2
اککھیلہےاورنگِسلیماںمرےنزدیک
اکباتہےاعجازِمسیحامرےآگے
Ik khel hai aurang-e-Sulaimaan mere nazdeek
Ik baat hai e'jaaz-e-Maseeha mere aage
The throne of Solomon is but a game in my view,
The miracle of Messiah is but a trifle before me.

Couplet 3
جزنامِجنوںکچھنہیںسینہمیںمرےاور
گرہےتوفقطغبارِصحرامرےآگے
Juz naam-e-junoon kuchh nahin seena mein mere aur
Gar hai to faqat ghubaar-e-sehra mere aage
Except the name of madness, there's nothing in my breast,
If there is, it's merely the dust of the desert before me.

Couplet 4
ایماںمجھےروکےہےجوکھینچےہےمجھےکفر
کعبہمرےپیچھےہےکلیسامرےآگے
Eemaan mujhe roke hai jo kheencha hai mujhe kufr
Ka'ba mere peeche hai Kaleesa mere aage
Faith holds me back, while infidelity draws me near,
The Kaaba is behind me, the Church is before me.

Couplet 5
پھردیکھیےاندازِگُلافشانیِگفتار
رکھدےکوئیپیمانۂصہبامرےآگے
Phir dekhiye andaaz-e-gul-afshaani-e-guftaar
Rakh de koi paimaana-e-sehba mere aage
Then observe the manner of my speech, scattering flowers,
If someone places a goblet of wine before me.

Couplet 6
غالبؔترااحوالوغمِہجرووصال
سبسےبیاںہوجورُخِلیلیٰمرےآگے
Ghalib! tera ahwaal-o-gham-e-hijr-o-wisaal
Sab se bayaan ho jo rukh-e-Laila mere aage
Ghalib! Your state and the grief of separation and union,
Let it all be revealed if Layla's face is before me.
