Aao ba-sair-e-gulshan-e-zar-o-gul

آؤ بَسیرِ گلشنِ زر و گُل

By Mirza Ghalib

Couplet 1
آؤبَسیرِگلشنِزروگُل
(غالبکےکسیمستنددیوانمیںیہغزلدستیابنہیںہے)
Aao ba-sair-e-gulshan-e-zar-o-gul
(Ghalib ke kisi mustanad divan mein ye ghazal dastiyab nahin hai)
Come, let's wander in the garden of gold and flowers.
(This ghazal is not available in any authenticated Divan of Ghalib.)
