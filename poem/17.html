Na hui gar mere marne se tasalli na sahi

نہ ہوئی گر مرے مرنے سے تسلی نہ سہی

By Mirza Ghalib

Couplet 1
نہہوئیگرمرےمرنےسےتسلی،نہسہی
امتحاںاوربھیباقیہوتویہبھینہسہی
Na hui gar mere marne se tasalli, na sahi
Imtihan aur bhi baqi ho to ye bhi na sahi
If my death brought no solace, then so be it.
If there are more trials remaining, then this too, so be it.

Couplet 2
کیاغمِمرگکہوہبھیشبِہجراںگزری
یعنیاسدردمیںآرامبھیتھا،یہبھینہسہی
Kya gham-e-marg ke woh bhi shab-e-hijran guzri
Ya'ni is dard mein aaram bhi tha, ye bhi na sahi
What grief is there in death, for even that passed like a night of separation?
Meaning, there was solace even in that pain; if not, then so be it.

Couplet 3
دلجلاناہیسہیرسمِوفا،ہاںیوںہی
یہبھینذرِآتشِشعلہنوا،یہبھینہسہی
Dil jalana hi sahi rasm-e-wafa, haan yun hi
Ye bhi nazr-e-aatish-e-shola-nawa, ye bhi na sahi
Burning the heart, so be it, is the custom of loyalty, yes, just like that.
Let this too be an offering to the fire of the eloquent flame; if not, then so be it.

Couplet 4
تیرگیہےکہہےنورِسحروشامِفراق
نورِخورشیدبھیکیا،شامِغریباںنہسہی
Teergi hai ke hai noor-e-sehar o shaam-e-firaq
Noor-e-khursheed bhi kya, shaam-e-ghariban na sahi
Is it darkness, or the light of dawn and evening of separation?
What good is the light of the sun, if it's not the evening of the stranger/lonely one?

Couplet 5
کونہوتاہےجوہرشعلہنواکوجلائے
کونجلتاہے،یہاندازِوفا،یہبھینہسہی
Kaun hota hai jo har shola-nawa ko jalaye
Kaun jalta hai, ye andaaz-e-wafa, ye bhi na sahi
Who is it that ignites every eloquent flame?
Who gets burnt? This manner of loyalty, if not, then so be it.

Couplet 6
نازواندازِسخن،طرزِادا،ہائےغضب
آہ!وہحسنِسراپاکہوہجلوہنہسہی
Naaz-o-andaaz-e-sukhan, tarz-e-ada, haay ghazab
Aah! woh husn-e-sarapa ke woh jalwa na sahi
The airs and style of speech, the manner of grace, oh, what calamity!
Alas! That perfect beauty, if not that splendor, then so be it.

Couplet 7
عمربھرکاش!نہہوتےہماہلِوفا
کیاکہیںوہبھینہسہی،اوریہبھینہسہی
Umar bhar kaash! na hote hum ahl-e-wafa
Kya kahein woh bhi na sahi, aur ye bhi na sahi
Oh, if only we hadn't been people of loyalty our whole lives!
What can we say? If not that, then so be it; and if not this, then so be it.
