Har ek baat pe kehte ho tum ke tu kya hai

ہر ایک بات پہ کہتے ہو تم کہ تو کیا ہے

By Mirza Ghalib

Couplet 1
ہرایکباتپہکہتےہوتمکہتوکیاہے
تمہیںکہوکہیہاندازِگفتگوکیاہے
Har ek baat pe kehte ho tum ke tu kya hai
Tumhein kaho ke yeh andaaz-e-guftago kya hai
At every word you say to me, 'What are you?'
You tell me, what style of conversation is this?

Couplet 2
چپکرہاہےبدنپرلہوسےپیراہن
ہماریجیبکوابحاجتِرفوکیاہے
Chipak raha hai badan par lahoo se pairahan
Hamari jeb ko ab haajat-e-rafu kya hai
My shirt is sticking to my body because of my blood;
What need is there now to stitch or mend my collar?

Couplet 3
رگوںمیںدوڑتےپھرنےکےہمنہیںقائل
جبآنکھہیسےنہٹپکاتوپھرلہوکیاہے
Ragon mein daudte phirne ke hum nahi qaayel
Jab aankh hi se na tapka to phir lahoo kya hai
I am not convinced by blood that merely flows inside the veins;
If it does not drip from the eyes as tears, then what use is it as blood?

Couplet 4
رہینہطاقتِگفتاراوراگرہوبھی
توکسامیدپہکہیےکہآرزوکیاہے
Rahi na taaqat-e-guftaar aur agar ho bhi
To kis umeed pe kahiye ke aarzoo kya hai
No strength of speech remains, and even if it did;
On what hope should I speak and declare what my desire is?

Couplet 5
ہواہےشہکامصاحبپھرےہےاتراتا
ورنہشہرمیںغالبکیآبروکیاہے
Hua hai shah ka musahib phire hai itrata
Varna shehar mein Ghalib ki aabroo kya hai
Now that he has become a companion to the King, Ghalib walks around proudly;
Otherwise, what real honor or standing does he have in this city?
