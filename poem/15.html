Muddat hui hai yaar ko mehman kiye hue

مدت ہوئی ہے یار کو مہمان کیے ہوئے

By Mirza Ghalib

Couplet 1
مدتہوئیہےیارکومہمانکیےہوئے
جوشِقدحسےبزمچراغاںکیےہوئے
Muddat hui hai yaar ko mehman kiye hue
Josh-e-qadah se bazm charaghan kiye hue
A long time has passed since I hosted my beloved.
Since I illuminated the gathering with the passion of the wine-cup.

Couplet 2

Baaz ik bheer si padne lagi hai dil mein
Hai isi koocha-o-bazaar mein samaan kiye hue
Again, a crowd begins to gather in my heart,
While my beloved makes preparations in this very street and market.

Couplet 3
گرمیِشوقِنظارہنےنہبخشیمہلت
صدرہآئینہخانہکوحیرانکیےہوئے
Garmi-e-shauq-e-nazaara ne na bakhshi mohlat
Sad rah aaina-khana ko hairan kiye hue
The fervor of the desire to behold did not grant me respite,
Leaving a hundred mirror-houses in bewildered amazement.

Couplet 4
کُوبہکُوپھیلگئیباتشناسائیکی

Koo ba koo phail gai baat shanaasaai ki
Us ne parde mein bhi rakha mujhe uriyaan kiye hue
The talk of our acquaintance spread from street to street,
Even while in veil, she kept me exposed and bare.

Couplet 5
چشمِبددوروہگنجینہمعنیٰہےکہجو
دلکےخمیازوےکوخنداںکیےہوئے
Chashm-e-bad door woh ganjina-e-ma'ani hai ke jo
Dil ke khamyaze ko khandaan kiye hue
May evil eye be far, that treasure of meaning (my beloved) is such
That even the yawning of the heart (its weariness) is made to smile.

Couplet 6
پھروُہیشوخیورندیہےہمارےبسمیں
پھروُہیدلکوجگرتشنہِسوزاںکیےہوئے
Phir wuhi shokhi-o-rindi hai hamare bas mein
Phir wuhi dil ko jigar tishna-e-sozan kiye hue
Again, the same mischief and revelry are within my control,
Again, the same heart and liver are made thirsty and burning.

Couplet 7
ہمکومعلومہےجنتکیحقیقتلیکن
دلکےبہلانےکوغالبیہخیالاچھاہے
Hum ko ma'aloom hai jannat ki haqeeqat lekin
Dil ke behlane ko Ghalib yeh khayal achha hai
We know the reality of heaven, but
To amuse the heart, Ghalib, this thought is good.
