Ibne-Maryam hua kare koi

ابنِ مریم ہوا کرے کوئی

By Mirza Ghalib

Couplet 1
ابنِمریمہواکرےکوئی
میرےدردکیدواکرےکوئی
Ibne-Maryam hua kare koi
Mere dard ki dawa kare koi
Let anyone be the Son of Mary (Jesus, the miraculous healer);
Only if they can cure my pain will they matter to me.

Couplet 2
شرعوآئیںپرمدارسہی
ایسےقاتلکاکیاکرےکوئی
Shara-o-aaeen par madaar sahi
Aise qaatil ka kya kare koi
Admittedly, the society runs on law and religious code;
But what is one to do against a killer who is too beautiful to punish?

Couplet 3
بکرہاہوںجنوںمیںکیاکیاکچھ
کچھنہسمجھےخداکرےکوئی
Bak raha hoon junoon mein kya kya kuchh
Kuch na samjhe khuda kare koi
What nonsense I am babbling in this state of madness!
May God ensure that no one understands what I am saying.

Couplet 4
نہسنوگربراکہےکوئی
نہکہوگربراکرےکوئی
Na suno gar bura kahe koi
Na kaho gar bura kare koi
Do not listen if someone speaks ill of another;
Do not repeat it if someone does something wrong.
