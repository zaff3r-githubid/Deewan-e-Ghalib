Jahan-e-dagar-gun hai kya kijiye

جہاں دگرگوں ہے کیا کیجیے

By Mirza Ghalib

Couplet 1
جہاںدگرگوںہےکیاکیجیے
دلِبیدردکوکیاکیجیے
Jahan-e-dagar-gun hai kya kijiye
Dil-e-bedard ko kya kijiye
The world is in turmoil, what can be done?
What can be done with this insensitive heart?

Couplet 2
فلکنےسکھائیہےوحشتہمیں
غمِروزگارکوکیاکیجیے
Falak ne sikhayi hai vahshat hamein
Gham-e-rozgar ko kya kijiye
The heavens have taught us wildness/desolation;
What can one do with the sorrow of livelihood/life?

Couplet 3
نظرنہیںآتاکوئییارِآشنا
اناجنبیوںمیںکیاکیجیے
Nazar nahin aata koi yaar-e-ashna
In ajnabiyon mein kya kijiye
No familiar friend is seen;
What can one do amidst these strangers?

Couplet 4
یہبھیرسمہےکہخودگُذیدہہوں
اپنےآزارکوکیاکیجیے
Yeh bhi rasm hai ke khud guzida hoon
Apne aazar ko kya kijiye
It is also a custom that I am self-bitten (self-afflicted);
What can one do with one's own torment?

Couplet 5
جوپاسِناموسِمحفلنہیں
نگاہِبےحجابکوکیاکیجیے
Jo pas-e-namoos-e-mehfil nahin
Nigah-e-be-hijab ko kya kijiye
When there's no regard for the honor of the assembly,
What can one do with an unveiled gaze?

Couplet 6
اسیرِدامِبلاہوں'غالب'
سواہائےہائےکیاکیجیے
Aseer-e-daam-e-bala hoon 'Ghalib'
Siva haay haay kya kijiye
I am a captive in the snare of calamity, 'Ghalib';
Besides sighs of lamentation, what can one do?
