Rahiye ab aisi jagah chal kar jahan koi na ho

رہیے اب ایسی جگہ چل کر جہاں کوئی نہ ہو

By Mirza Ghalib

Couplet 1
رہیےابایسیجگہچلکرجہاںکوئینہہو
ہمسخنکوئینہہواورہمزباںکوئینہہو
Rahiye ab aisi jagah chal kar jahan koi na ho
Ham-sukhan koi na ho aur ham-zabaan koi na ho
Let me now go and reside in a place where there is no one,
Where there is no one to converse with, and no one to share a language with.

Couplet 2
بےدرودیوارسااکگھربنایاچاہیے
کوئیہمسایہنہہواورپاسباںکوئینہہو
Be-dar-o-diwaar sa ik ghar banaaya chaahiye
Koi hamsaaya na ho aur paasbaan koi na ho
I should build a house without doors or walls,
Where there is no neighbor, and no guard.

Couplet 3
پڑیےگربیمارتوکوئینہہوتیماردار
اوراگرمرجائیےتونوحہخواںکوئینہہو
Padiye gar beemaar to koi na ho teemaar-daar
Aur agar mar jaaiye to nauha-khwaan koi na ho
If I fall ill, let there be no one to nurse me,
And if I die, let there be no one to lament.

Couplet 4
بجھرہیہےآگدلکی،دَمبہدَم،جلتاہوںمیں
کوئیبھیمیرانہہواورمہرباںکوئینہہو
Bujh rahi hai aag dil ki, dam ba dam, jalta hoon main
Koi bhi mera na ho aur mehrbaan koi na ho
The fire of my heart is dying out, moment by moment, I burn,
Let no one be mine, and let there be no compassionate one.

Couplet 5
میںنےروکارات'غالب'کو،وہجاتےتھےجہاں
وہاگرجاتے،توکہتےکہیہاںکوئینہہو
Maine roka raat 'Ghalib' ko, woh jaate the jahan
Woh agar jaate, to kehte ke yahan koi na ho
Last night I stopped Ghalib, as he was going to a place,
If he were to go, he would say that there should be no one here.
