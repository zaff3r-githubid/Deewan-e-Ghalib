Aah ko chahiye ik umr asar hone tak

آہ کو چاہیے اک عمر اثر ہونے تک

By Mirza Ghalib

Couplet 1
آہکوچاہیےاکعمراثرہونےتک
کونجیتاہےتریزلفکےسرہونےتک
Aah ko chahiye ik umr asar hone tak
Kaun jeeta hai teri zulf ke sar hone tak
A sigh requires an age to take effect,
Who lives till your tresses are tamed?

Couplet 2
دامہرموجمیںہےحلقۂصدکامِنہنگ
دیکھیںکیاگزرےہےقطرےپہگہرہونےتک
Daam har mauj mein hai halqa-e sad kaam-e nahang
Dekhen kya guzre hai qatre pe guhar hone tak
In every wave's trap, there's a hundred-toothed crocodile's maw,
Let's see what a drop endures till it becomes a pearl.

Couplet 3
مہرِمحشرنہیںشکوہِوفااےظالم
ہمبھیمنہمیںزباںرکھتےہیںکاشپوچھوکہمدعاکیاہے
Mahr-e-mehshar nahin shikwa-e wafa ae zaalim
Ham bhi munh mein zabaan rakhte hain kaash poocho ke muddaa kya hai
This complaint of fidelity, O cruel one, is not for the Doomsday's reckoning.
We too have a tongue in our mouth, if only you'd ask what our intention is.

Couplet 4
ہمنےماناکہتغافلنہکروگےلیکن
خاکہوجائیںگےہمتمکوخبرہونےتک
Hum ne maana ke taghaful na karoge lekin
Khaak ho jayenge hum tum ko khabar hone tak
We concede that you won't neglect us eventually, but
We will turn to dust by the time you become aware.

Couplet 5
جلتاہےدلکہکیوںنہہمیکجاہوئے
یاربوہبھیہےکیاتماشائےدیرہونےتک
Jalta hai dil ke kyoon na hum yak jaa hue
Ya Rab woh bhi hai kya tamasha-e der hone tak
My heart burns, why did we not unite?
O Lord, what a spectacle it is, this prolonged delay!

Couplet 6

I concede that Ghalib is nothing,
But if he comes for free, what's wrong with that?
