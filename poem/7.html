Hazaron Khwahishen Aisi Ke Har Khwahish Pe Dam Nikle

ہزاروں خواہشیں ایسی کہ ہر خواہش پہ دم نکلے

By Mirza Ghalib

Couplet 1
ہزاروںخواہشیںایسیکہہرخواہشپہدمنکلے
بہتنکلےمرےارمانلیکنپھربھیکمنکلے
Hazaron khwahishen aisi ke har khwahish pe dam nikle
Bahut nikle mere armaan lekin phir bhi kam nikle
Thousands of desires, such that with each desire, life departs.
Many of my longings were fulfilled, yet still, they felt few.

Couplet 2
ڈرےکیوںمیراقاتلکیارہےگااسکیگردنپر
وہخوںجوچشمِترسےعمربھریوںدمبہدمنکلے
Dare kyoon mera qatil kya rahega uski gardan par
Woh khoon jo chashm-e-tar se umr bhar yoon dam-ba-dam nikle
Why should my killer be afraid? What will remain on his neck?
That blood which flows constantly, drop by drop, from my tearful eyes throughout life?

Couplet 3
نکلناخلدسےآدمکاسُنتےآئےہیںلیکن
بہتبےآبروہوکرترےکوچےسےہمنکلے
Nikalna khuld se Adam ka sunte aaye hain lekin
Bahut be-abroo ho kar tere kooche se hum nikle
We have heard of Adam's expulsion from Paradise,
But we departed from your lane in utter disgrace.

Couplet 4
بھرےہےیوںترےوعدےپرمیرادلکہاےظالم
قسماکاورکھااپنیاگرپیمانشکننکلے
Bhare hai yoon tere waade par mera dil ke aye zaalim
Qasam ek aur kha apni agar paimaan-shikan nikle
My heart is so filled with belief in your promises, O cruel one,
Take another oath by your own self, if I prove to be a breaker of promises.

Couplet 5
غرضکاٹیشبِہمنےکبھیدیوارگرپڑی
کبھیدرکواڑادیکھاکبھیچھتکوخمنکلے
Gharaz kaati shab-e-humne kabhi deewar gir padi
Kabhi dar ko uda dekha kabhi chhat ko kham nikle
In short, we passed our nights such that sometimes a wall collapsed,
Sometimes we saw the door swept away, sometimes the roof sagged.

Couplet 6
جہاںمیںہوسکےکیوںکرہراِککارِمسیحائی
جبایکآہِبےتاثیرہوسوسوکادمنکلے
Jahan mein ho sake kyoon kar har ek kaar-e-maseehai
Jab ek aah-e-be-taseer ho sau sau ka dam nikle
How can every act of healing be possible in this world,
When one ineffective sigh causes a hundred lives to depart?

Couplet 7
کہاںتکروؤںوہبیدادِرسوائیکہاسکےگھر
بہتبیدردتھاغالبؔمگربیدردکمنکلے
Kahan tak ro'oon woh bedaad-e-ruswai ke uske ghar
Bahut bedard tha Ghalibؔ magar bedard kam nikle
How long shall I weep over that injustice of dishonor at her house?
Ghalib was very heartless (or thought to be), but few proved to be truly heartless (or the heartless ones were fewer).
