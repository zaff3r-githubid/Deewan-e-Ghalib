Aish-e-nazara hai, gul-e-khush-e-rang nahin

عیشِ نظارہ ہے گلِ خوش رنگ نہیں

By Mirza Ghalib

Couplet 1
عیشِنظارہہےگلِخوشرنگنہیں
اےغافل،بےرُخیِشوقکہیہتنگنہیں
Aish-e-nazara hai, gul-e-khush-e-rang nahin
Ay ghafil, be-rukhi-e-shauq ki ye tang nahin
It is the delight of seeing, not a beautiful flower
O heedless one, it is the indifference of desire, for this is not a narrow path.

Couplet 2
عرضِنیازِعشقکےقابلنہیںدل
جسخاککوکہتےہیںکہیہسنگنہیں
Arz-e-niyaz-e-ishq ke qabil nahin dil
Jis khaak ko kehte hain ki ye sang nahin
The heart is not worthy of expressing the demands of love
This dust which they say is not a stone.

Couplet 3
حیفاسچارگرہکپڑےکیقسمتغافل
جسکیقسمتمیںہوپیراہنِیوسفؐنہیں
Haif us chaar girah kapRe ki qismat ghafil
Jis ki qismat mein ho pairahan-e-Yusuf nahin
Alas for the fate of that four-fold piece of cloth, O heedless one
Whose destiny does not hold the shirt of Yusuf.

Do not trouble yourself to inflict pain upon me
I do not make your memory colorless in my heart.

Couplet 5
وحشتِآتشِدلسےشبِتنہائیمیں
سایہمیرابھیمرےپاسنہتھاسنگنہیں
Wahshat-e-aatish-e-dil se shab-e-tanhai mein
Saya mera bhi mere paas na tha sang nahin
Due to the wildness of the heart's fire in the night of solitude
Even my shadow was not with me; it is not a stone.

Couplet 6
پھرمجھےدیکھکےاُسبُتنےکہامیںنےکہا
باندھےگاتوبھیمریجانکبھیسنگنہیں
Phir mujhe dekh ke us but ne kaha, maine kaha
Bandhega tu bhi meri jaan kabhi sang nahin
Again, seeing me, that idol said, and I said
You too, my soul, will never be bound by a stone.

Couplet 7
بےگانگیِخلقسےغالبنہڈرکہیہ
ہوتاہےسنگوہجوبہجاسنگنہیں
Begangi-e-khalq se Ghalib na dar ki ye
Hota hai sang woh jo ba-ja sang nahin
Do not fear the alienation of people, Ghalib, for this:
That which is (called) a stone is actually not a stone by its nature.
