Gham-e-hasti ka Asad kis se ho juz marg ilaj

غم ہستی کا اسدؔ کس سے ہو جز مرگ علاج

By Mirza Ghalib

Couplet 1
غمہستیکااسدؔکسسےہوجزمرگعلاج
شمعہررنگمیںجلتیہےسحرہونےتک
Gham-e-hasti ka Asad kis se ho juz marg ilaaj
Shama har rang mein jalti hai sahar hone tak
Oh Asad, who else can cure the sorrow of existence, save death?
The candle, in every hue, burns until dawn.

Couplet 2
اشکفرقتمیںہےیاربمرےدیدۂخوںناب
سلسلہموجکااکدیدۂحیراںہونےتک
Ashk-e-furqat mein hai ya rab mere deedah-e-khoon naab
Silsila mauj ka ik deedah-e-hairaan hone tak
Oh Lord, in the tears of separation, my blood-filled eyes
Are a continuous wave, until they become a bewildered gaze.

Couplet 3
پھروہیسامنےآنکھوںکےہےنقشِخیال
لےگیاتھاجسےمیںمحوِتماشاہونےتک
Phir wahi saamne aankhon ke hai naqsh-e-khayal
Le gaya tha jise main mahv-e-tamasha hone tak
Again, the same image of thought is before my eyes,
Which I had taken away, lost in spectacle, until it vanished.

Couplet 4
تھاترےغممیںگراںگردشِشاموسحر
اورگھڑیپھرتیرہیبارہپہجاہونےتک
Tha tere gham mein garan gardish-e-shaam-o-sahar
Aur ghari phirti rahi baarah pe ja hone tak
In your sorrow, the turning of dusk and dawn felt heavy,
And the clock hand kept moving until it reached twelve.

Couplet 5

Let such intense desire always avoid interference (or control),
Let the tearful eye become my bewildered gaze until then.

Couplet 6
کچھنہیںغیرازپریشانیٔخاطرحاصل
دلصدپارہکاہررشتہرگِجاںہونےتک
Kuchh nahin ghair az pareshaani-e-khaatir haasil
Dil-e-sad paara ka har rishta rag-e-jaan hone tak
Nothing is achieved but distress of the heart,
Until every strand of the hundred-pieced heart becomes a vein of life.

Couplet 7
اسدؔوہشوخستارہجبیںگروہاںہے
توپھریہچشمکاخترنہیںتواورکیاہے
Asad! Woh shokh sitara jabeen gar wahan hai
To phir yeh chashmak-e-akhtar nahin to aur kya hai
Asad, if that mischievous, star-browed one is there,
Then what is this, if not the twinkling of stars?
