Aashiqui sabr-talab aur tamanna betaab

عاشقی صبر طلب اور تمنا بی تاب

By Mirza Ghalib

Couplet 1
عاشقیصبرطلباورتمنابیتاب
دلکاکیارنگکروںخونجگرہونےتک
Aashiqui sabr-talab aur tamanna betaab
Dil ka kya rang karoon khoon-e-jigar hone tak
Love demands patience, but desire is restless;
What hue shall I paint my heart until it turns to blood?

Couplet 2
ہمنےماناکہتغافلنہکروگےلیکن
خاکہوجائیںگےہمتمکوخبرہونےتک
Humne maana ke taghaful na karoge lekin
Khaak ho jaayenge hum tum ko khabar hone tak
I concede that you will not neglect me forever, but
I will turn to dust by the time you become aware.

Couplet 3
پرتوخورشیدعالمتابسےشبنمکوفنا
تجھکوبھیاےبےخبریکچھنہیںہونےکاخیال
Partav-e-khursheed-e-alam-taab se shabnam ko fana
Tujh ko bhi ay be-khabari kuch nahin hone ka khayaal
Just as the dewdrop perishes from the world-illuminating sun's ray,
So too, O unaware one, you have no thought of anything happening (to me).

Couplet 4
یکنظربیشنہیںفرصتہستیغافل
گرمیبزمہےاکرقصشررہونےتک
Yak nazar besh nahin fursat-e-hasti ghafil
Garmi-e-bazm hai ik raqs-e-sharar hone tak
O heedless one, the span of existence is no more than a single glance;
The warmth of the gathering is but the dance of a spark until it extinguishes.

Couplet 5
غمہستیکااسدؔکسسےہوجزمرگعلاج
شمعہررنگمیںجلتیہےسحرہونےتک
Gham-e-hasti ka Asadؔ kis se ho juz marg ilaaj
Shama' har rang mein jalti hai sahar hone tak
O Asad, by whom but death can the sorrow of existence be cured?
The candle burns in every hue until dawn breaks.
