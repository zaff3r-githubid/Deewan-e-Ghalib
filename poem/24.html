Dil hi to hai na sang-o-khisht dard se bhar na aaye kyun

دل ہی تو ہے نہ سنگ و خشت درد سے بھر نہ آئے کیوں

By Mirza Ghalib

Couplet 1
دلہیتوہےنہسنگوخشتدردسےبھرنہآئےکیوں
روئیںگےہمہزاربارکوئیہمیںستائےکیوں
Dil hi to hai na sang-o-khisht, dard se bhar na aaye kyun
Royenge hum hazar baar, koi hamein sataaye kyun
It is but a heart, not a brick or stone; why shouldn't it be filled with pain?
I shall cry a thousand times; why should anyone torment me?

Couplet 2
قیدِحیاتوبندِغماصلمیںدونوںایکہیں
موتسےپہلےآدمیغمسےنجاتپائےکیوں
Qaid-e-hayat o band-e-gham asal mein dono ek hain
Maut se pehle aadmi gham se nijaat paaye kyun
The prison of life and the bondage of sorrow, in reality, are one and the same;
Before death, why should a person ever find liberation from grief?

Couplet 3
ہاںوہنہیںخداپرستجاؤوہبےوفاسہی
تمبھیتومیرےیارکوچھڑائےکیوں
Haan woh nahin Khuda parast, jaao woh bewafa sahi
Tum bhi toh mere yaar ko chhudaye kyun
Yes, they are not God-fearing, let them be faithless;
But why would you try to liberate my beloved?

Couplet 4
غمگرچہجاںگسلہےپہکہاںبچیںکہدلہے
عشقِجنوںپیشہکااسکوروارکھےکیوں
Gham garche jaan-gusal hai, pe kahan bachein ke dil hai
Ishq-e-junooN pesha ka, isko rawa rakhe kyun
Though sorrow is soul-destroying, how can one escape it, when there is a heart?
Why should it allow the profession of madness (love) to continue?

Couplet 5
غالببرانہمانجوواعظبراکہے
ایسابھیکوئیہےکہسباچھاکہلائےکیوں
Ghalib bura na maan jo wa'iz bura kahe
Aisa bhi koi hai ke sab achchha kehlaaye kyun
Ghalib, don't take it ill if the preacher speaks ill of you;
Is there anyone at all who is praised by everyone? (Why should anyone be called good by all?)
