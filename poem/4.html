Ye na thi hamari qismat ke wisal-e-yaar hota

یہ نہ تھی ہماری قسمت کہ وصالِ یار ہوتا

By Mirza Ghalib

Couplet 1
یہنہتھیہماریقسمتکہوصالِیارہوتا
اگراورجیتےرہتےیہیانتظارہوتا
Ye na thi hamari qismat ke wisal-e-yaar hota
Agar aur jeete rehte yehi intezar hota
It was not in my destiny that union with the beloved would happen;
Even if I had lived longer, it would have been the same waiting.

Couplet 2
ترےوعدےپرجیےہمتویہجانجھوٹجانا
کہخوشیسےمرنہجاتےاگراعتبارہوتا
Tere vaade par jiye hum to ye jaan jhooth jaana
Ke khushi se mar na jaate agar aitbaar hota
If I lived on your promise, know that I knew it to be a lie;
For would I not have died of joy, had I actually believed it?

Couplet 3
کہوںکسسےمیںکہکیاہےشبِغمبریبلاہے
مجھےکیابراتھامرنااگرایکبارہوتا
Kahoon kis se main ke kya hai shab-e-gham buri bala hai
Mujhe kya bura tha marna agar ek baar hota
To whom shall I say what the night of sorrow is—it is a terrible curse!
I would not have minded dying, if only it happened just once.

Couplet 4
کوئیمیرےدلسےپوچھےترےتیرِنیمکشکو
یہخلشکہاںسےہوتیجوجگرکےپارہوتا
Koi mere dil se pooche tere teer-e-neem-kash ko
Ye khalish kahaan se hoti jo jigar ke paar hota
Ask my heart about your half-drawn arrow;
Where would this sweet prickling be, had it passed completely through the liver?

Couplet 5
یہمسائلِتصوف،یہتیرابیانغالب
تجھےہمولیسمجھتےجونہبادہخوارہوتا
Yeh masaail-e-tasawwuf, yeh tera bayaan Ghalib
Tujhe hum wali samajhte jo na baada-khwar hota
These complex matters of mysticism, and this eloquent explanation of yours, O Ghalib!
We would have considered you a saint, had you not been a drinker of wine.
