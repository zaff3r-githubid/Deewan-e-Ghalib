Koi Umeed Bar Nahin Aati

کوئی امید بر نہیں آتی

By Mirza Ghalib

Couplet 1
کوئیامیدبرنہیںآتی
کوئیصورتنظرنہیںآتی
Koi umeed bar nahin aati
Koi soorat nazar nahin aati
No hope finds fulfillment
No path seems visible

Couplet 2
موتکاایکدنمعینہے

Maut ka ek din mu'ayyan hai
Neend kyun raat bhar nahin aati
Death has a fixed day
Why then does sleep not come all night?

Couplet 3
آگےآتیتھیحالِدلپہہنسی
ابکسیباتپرنہیںآتی
Aage aati thi haal-e dil pe hansi
Ab kisi baat par nahin aati
Before, I used to laugh at the state of my heart
Now, I don't laugh at anything

Couplet 4
ہےکچھایسیہیباتجوچپہوں
ورنہکیاباتکرنہیںآتی
Hai kuchch aisi hi baat jo chup hoon
Varna kya baat kar nahin aati
There is certainly something (significant) that makes me silent
Otherwise, is it that I don't know how to speak?

Couplet 5
بارباراُسکےدرپہجاتاہوں
ابیہعادتبھیگھرنہیںآتی
Baar baar us ke dar pe jaata hoon
Ab ye aadat bhi ghar nahin aati
Again and again, I go to his door
Now, even this habit doesn't return home (i.e., doesn't stop)

Couplet 6
کچھتوپڑھئےکہلوگکہتےہیں
آجغالبؔغزلسرانہیںآتی
Kuchh to paṛhiye ke log kehte hain
Aaj Ghalib ghazal-sara nahin aati
Recite something, for people say
Today, Ghalib cannot compose a ghazal
