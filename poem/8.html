Aamad-e-khat se hua hai sard bazar-e-dost

آمدِ خط سے ہوا ہے سرد بازارِ دوست

By Mirza Ghalib

Couplet 1
آمدِخطسےہواہےسردبازارِدوست
چہرہجوںمہتابتھا،ابہوگیارخسارِدوست
Aamad-e-khat se hua hai sard bazar-e-dost
Chehra jun mahtab tha, ab ho gaya rukhsar-e-dost
With the advent of the down-fuzz, the beloved's market has grown cold.
The face was like the full moon, now it has become the cheek of the beloved.

Couplet 2
اےدلِناداں!تِریاُلجھیہےزُلفِیارمیں
پیچوخممیںسربسرہے،ہرایکتارِدوست
Ae dil-e-naadaan! teri uljhi hai zulf-e-yaar mein
Pech-o-kham mein sar basar hai, har ek taar-e-dost
O foolish heart! You are entangled in the beloved's locks.
Every single strand of the beloved's hair is entirely full of twists and turns.

Couplet 3
اےپری!تیراتبسمبےسببپُرشوقہے
کردیاہےتونےجو،بےکارِہشیارِدوست
Ae pari! Tera tabassum be-sabab pur-shauq hai
Kar diya hai tu ne jo, be-kaar-e-hushiyar-e-dost
O fairy! Your smile is needlessly full of desire/charm.
For you have rendered useless the vigilance of the wary lover.

Couplet 4
اےبتِسنگین!جوتونےدللیاہےمیرا
عاشقوںکےگلےمیںہےیہزنجیرِدوست
Ae but-e-sangīn! jo tu ne dil liya hai merā
Āshiqoñ ke gale meñ hai ye zanjīr-e-dost
O stony idol! The heart you have taken from me
Is this very chain around the necks of lovers.

Couplet 5
دردِدلکیحکایتکیاکہوںیاروںکو
ہےمراسینہ،بَنگیازخمِبَهارِدوست
Dard-e-dil ki hikayat kya kahoon yaaron ko
Hai mera seena, ban gaya zakhm-e-bahaar-e-dost
What tale of heartache can I tell my friends?
My chest has become, a wound of the beloved's spring.

Couplet 6
جوشِوحشتسےبَپاہےشورشِغمہرطرف
کسطرحسےآجکلہےانتظارِدوست
Josh-e-wahshat se bapa hai shorish-e-gham har taraf
Kis tarah se aaj kal hai intezar-e-dost
A tumult of grief has erupted everywhere due to the intensity of madness.
How difficult it is to endure the waiting for the beloved these days!

Couplet 7
غالبؔ!ابکچھغمنہیںگریارنےچھوڑیوفا
دیکھتو!پروانہہےشمعِگلزارِدوست
Ghalib! Ab kuchh gham nahin gar yaar ne chhodi wafa
Dekh to! Parwana hai shama-e-gulzar-e-dost
Ghalib! Now there is no sorrow if the beloved has abandoned loyalty.
Just look! The moth is the candle of the beloved's garden.
