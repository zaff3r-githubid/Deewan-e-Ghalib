Poem of the Day

Aashna sanjeeda-e-mausam nahin

آشنا سنجیدۂ موسم نہیں

By Mirza Ghalib

Couplet 1
آشناسنجیدۂموسمنہیں
دردِدلکچھآجبھیکمنہیں
Aashna sanjeeda-e-mausam nahin
Dard-e-dil kuch aaj bhi kam nahin
The familiar (friend/world) is not serious about the season (of life/suffering),
The pain of the heart is not less even today.

Couplet 2
ہےرگِہرسنگِریزہجوہرِ
پیکرِانساںکوآدمکمنہیں
Hai rag-e-har sang-reza jauhar-e
Paikar-e-insaan ko Adam kam nahin
Every pebble's vein holds a gem-like essence,
For the human form, Adam is not less (than a gem).

Couplet 3
کیاکروںگیمیںتریمےخواریوں
ساقیایہرسمِتوشہکمنہیں
Kya karungi main teri mai-khwariyon
Saqiya ye rasm-e-toshah kam nahin
What will I do with your wine-drinking?
O Saqi, this tradition of sustenance is not less (important).

Couplet 4
میںچلاجاتاہوںہرلمحےمیںدور
قافلۂدلمیںتوہمدمنہیں
Main chala jata hoon har lamhe mein duur
Qaafila-e-dil mein to hamdam nahin
I keep moving further away with every moment,
In the caravan of the heart, there is no companion for me.

Couplet 5
ہوسِرشکِمسیحانہگماںہےاسپر

Couplet 6
غالبؔآغوشمیںہےجسکوخیال
تنگیِآغوشِعالمکمنہیں
Ghalibؔ aaghosh mein hai jis ko khayaal
Tangī-e-aaghosh-e-aalam kam nahin
Ghalib! He who has thought (reflection) in his embrace,
For him, the narrowness of the world's embrace is not less (problematic).
